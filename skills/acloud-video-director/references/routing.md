# Production routing

Select the route by intended deliverable:

| Route | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| `single_scene` | One self-contained scene, card, announcement, title, or short visual unit |
| `short_form` | A vertical or square social video with a hook and payoff, normally under 90 seconds |
| `long_form` | A multi-section landscape production requiring sustained narrative structure |
| `drag_drop_animation` | User-arranged assets and text become a motion composition without generated footage |
| `documentary_montage` | Sourced real-world footage carries the story and every asset needs provenance |
| `product_demo` | A product, website, workflow, or feature is demonstrated with owned captures |
| `podcast_repurpose` | Existing long-form audio/video is cut into reviewed derivative clips |
| `faceless_video` | Narration, typography, diagrams, stills, stock, or generated assets carry the video |

Do not infer a premium generation provider from a route. A `faceless_video` may use only local narration, stills, HyperFrames motion, and FFmpeg.

When more than one route fits, choose the route that matches the primary output and represent secondary deliverables in `outputs`. Create separate linked specs when outputs require different timelines or approval histories.
